riphome privacy policy

riphome is used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account and devices.

What it accesses: the owner's Google Nest devices (camera streams, doorbell and thermostat information) through Google's Smart Device Management API, after the owner signs in with Google.

Where data goes: to the owner's own Home Assistant server at home, which shows camera pictures on a screen in the owner's home. Nothing is sold, shared with, or sent to anyone else. No analytics or advertising.

Removing access: the owner can remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact: the developer email shown on Google's sign-in screen for this app.
